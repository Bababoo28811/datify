How Datify works

Datify collects real, currently-running date deals in Singapore and helps you build an evening around them. Here's exactly what that means — including the bits we haven't built yet.

Where the deals come from

Two places. Some are curated — we find them on public listings, check they're still running, and record the real price, the outlet address and the expiry date. Others come straight from partner merchants who list with us directly.

Every curated deal links back to where it was published so you can verify it yourself, and we keep the fine print in. If a deal needs a particular credit card or only runs on weekday lunches, that's written into the description — not buried.

Find something you like

Explore is the full list, filterable by category and price. Swipe is the same deals one card at a time — right to shortlist, left to pass.

You don't need an account to shortlist. Tap the heart and it's saved to your browser straight away. Make an account later and everything you saved comes with you.

Build the date

Tell the planner your budget, how long you've got, the area you want to be in and the kind of night you're after. It picks deals that genuinely fit — it won't hand you a $25 dinner on a $15 budget, or a four-hour plan when you said three.

If your budget won't stretch, it says so and names the cheapest thing we actually have, then builds you a free evening instead — a walk along the Marina Bay waterfront, the Botanic Gardens, Fort Canning. Those are real places, and they're genuinely free.

What we don't do yet

We show the straight-line distance between stops, not travel time. A 7km hop might be twenty minutes or fifty depending on the MRT, so check the route before you commit.

Stop lengths are a flat estimate of an hour each — we don't yet know how long a given deal actually takes. And most photos on curated deals are stock images for illustration, not pictures of that venue. We say so on every deal page.

We'd rather tell you what's an estimate than dress a guess up as a fact.

If you run a business

Merchants can list their own deals. You get a dashboard, a Seller ID and control over your listings. Every deal — new or edited — goes through a quick review before it appears publicly, so nothing goes live unchecked.
